Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how Chakra-fin ("the app", "we") accesses, uses, stores and shares your data when you connect your Google account.

1. Data we access

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app asks for this scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly: read-only access to your Gmail messages.

The app only searches for bank and payment transaction alert emails (for example, messages containing "debited"). It does not read, process or keep any other email.

2. How we use the data

  • From matching emails we extract the transaction amount, payee/merchant, date, subject, sender, a short text snippet and the Gmail message ID.
  • We use this data only to build and show your personal expense records and categories, and to send you notifications about your own transactions.
  • We never send, change or delete your emails.

3. Storage and security

  • Extracted transaction data and OAuth tokens are stored in the app's private database and server, which only the app operator can access.
  • OAuth tokens are used only to call the Gmail API for you.
  • We take reasonable steps to protect the data from unauthorized access.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your Google user data with third parties. We do not use it for advertising. Transaction text may be processed by an automated categorization service only to sort your own expenses, and is not used for anything else.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Chakra-fin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Transaction data is kept until you ask us to delete it or stop using the app.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To ask for all of your stored data to be deleted, email the address below.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
